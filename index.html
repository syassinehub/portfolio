Research

Understanding nitrogen, protecting water

My research focuses on the impacts of agricultural activities and climate change on water quality and nitrogen biogeochemical processes, at field and watershed scales.

I use crop models and distributed agro-hydrological models to assess how land use and farming practices shape nitrogen dynamics across rural landscapes, under current and future climate and agricultural scenarios.

  • Climate change

    Impacts on farming systems and adaptation strategies.

  • Water quality

    Integrated environmental assessment, nitrogen transfers and eutrophication.

  • Modelling

    Crop and hydrological models, from field to landscape.

  • Territorial LCA

    Life cycle assessment applied to agricultural territories.

  • Decision support

    Tools to inform policy makers and local stakeholders.
